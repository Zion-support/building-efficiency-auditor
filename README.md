# 🏢 Building Efficiency Auditor

AI audits of HVAC, lighting and building envelope — turning raw telemetry into ranked retrofit opportunities. Part of the **Zion Energy & Facilities AI Suite (Batch 72)**.

## What it does
- Ingests BMS/IoT data, utility bills and weather to baseline building performance
- Detects scheduling, simultaneous heating/cooling, and envelope losses
- Ranks measures by savings, cost and payback; exports audit reports (ASHRAE-style)

## Key features
- Portfolio benchmarking across sites
- Measurement & verification (M&V) tracking after retrofits
- Feeds [Energy Consumption Forecaster](https://github.com/Zion-support/energy-consumption-forecaster) for savings validation
- Pairs with [Solar ROI Optimizer](https://github.com/Zion-support/solar-roi-optimizer) for on-site generation cases
